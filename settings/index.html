Audio tags

Each prayer trigger carries these as tags. In your Flow’s “cast a URL” action you pick which one to play — the app fills each from the URLs below. Leave a field blank to fall back to a built-in default (adhan) or to send nothing (adhkar/custom).

The Quran tag = this reciter's full recording of this surah.

Prayer Volume

Drag to set volume per prayer. Tap × to use the device default.

How to wire a Flow (one time per prayer)

  1. WHEN → “At any prayer time”, or “Specific Prayer” → pick one.
  2. THEN → your speaker app’s “Set Volume” action → insert [Volume %].
  3. Add another THEN → cast URL action (e.g. Chromecast → “Cast a sound URL”) → insert the tag you want: [Full adhan URL], [Quran URL], [Adhkar URL], etc.
