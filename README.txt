Prayers Alert — Islamic Prayer Times for Homey

Prayers Alert brings accurate Islamic prayer time notifications to your Homey smart home. Get audio adhan alerts, automate flows around prayer times, and stay connected to your daily worship schedule.

FEATURES
- Accurate prayer time calculations using multiple calculation methods
- Audio adhan playback at prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
- Prayer Times Widget — see today's prayer schedule at a glance on your Homey dashboard
- Hijri calendar support with Islamic occasion tracking
- Flow triggers for every prayer time with before/after offset support
- Flow conditions based on prayer name and Islamic occasions
- Highly configurable: calculation method, location, time adjustments, and more

FLOW CARDS
Triggers: Prayer time reached, prayer with offset (before/after), Hijri date events, Islamic occasions
Conditions: Prayer name check, Islamic occasion check

SETUP
1. Install the app
2. Open Settings and set your location or coordinates
3. Choose your preferred calculation method
4. Configure audio and notification preferences

SUPPORTED PRAYERS
Fajr · Sunrise · Dhuhr · Asr · Maghrib · Isha
